## About

Caribbean Fishing Academy Charters is a fishing charter and bay tour operation based in
San Juan, Puerto Rico. Trips are led by Captain Luis Burgos, a USCG certified captain and
the IGFA (International Game Fish Association) representative for Puerto Rico.

Charters run in support of the nonprofit Caribbean Fishing Academy (CFA), founded in 2013.
CFA teaches Puerto Rico's youth life skills, family integration, and marine conservation
through fishing. Booking a charter helps fund the youth outreach program. CFA has reached
over 5,000 kids in Puerto Rico since 2013.

## Location

## Fishing charter pricing

All fishing charters are private, for one group only. Prices start at:

- Reef fishing: from $610
- Half day inshore (4 hours, 1 to 6 guests): from $585, the flagship package
- Half day offshore (4 hours, 1 to 5 guests): from $699
- Three quarter day inshore (6 hours): from $685
- Three quarter day offshore (6 hours, up to 5 guests): from $899
- Full day offshore (8 hours): from $1,100

Combo add-ons (combining trip types or extra time) are available for $100 more.

## Species

Depending on the trip and the season, guests can catch tarpon, snook, jack crevalle, king
mackerel, snapper, mahi mahi, tuna, wahoo, blue marlin, white marlin, sailfish, and sharks.

## What is included on fishing trips

Included: USCG certified captain, fuel, tackle, bait (subject to availability), lures,
water, ice, fishing license, and light snacks.

Not included: gratuities for the crew.

## Sunset and bay cruises

Sunset and bay cruises start from $595, for 1 to 6 guests, 3 hours, and include free
appetizers and local drinks. Guests choose between two boats:

- "Sail La Vie," a 1994 Hunter sailboat, 37.5 feet
- "Game Day," a 2014 Contender power boat, 25 feet

The cruise route covers Old San Juan Bay, passing El Morro Castle, La Fortaleza, and the
Bacardi Factory.

## Booking policy

Every trip is a private charter for one group only, no shared boats. Booking is instant
confirmation, first come first served, no hassle. Deposits are refundable if the trip is
canceled 30 or more days in advance, or if the captain cancels for safety or weather
reasons.

## The nonprofit: Caribbean Fishing Academy (CFA)

CFA's mission is to teach Puerto Rico's youth healthy life skills, family integration, and
marine conservation through fishing. Founded in 2013, CFA has reached over 5,000 kids in
Puerto Rico. Charter bookings directly help fund this outreach program.
